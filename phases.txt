├── memories

├── events

   ↓

Phase 4 — Move Simulation Orchestration

This is one of the most important phases.

Current system:

Browser simulation loop
      ↓
CitizenAgent
      ↓
event wakeup / timer

The audit describes this as only partial compared with Emergence's round-robin orchestration and reaction system.

Build:

SimulationOrchestrator
        │
        ├── simulation clock
        ├── turn scheduler
        ├── agent scheduler
        ├── event queue
        ├── action execution
        └── world-state updates

Initially use:

CONCURRENT_AGENTS = 1

So:

Tick
 ↓
Ben
 ↓
execute
 ↓
world update
 ↓
Julie
 ↓
execute
 ↓
world update
 ↓
next tick

This will also make debugging Ben/Julie behavior much easier.

The key rule

Don't do this:

"Rewrite CiviGenis according to Emergence"

Do this:

Current CiviGenis
      ↓
Preserve working systems
      ↓
Add backend authority
      ↓
Add persistence
      ↓
Move orchestration
      ↓
Connect frontend
      ↓
Add Emergence systems one by one

That matches the audit's conclusion that CiviGenis can be evolved incrementally and that the navigation, map editor, 3D pipeline, tools, identity and perception should be preserved.

Your immediate next task should therefore be Phase 1 only: FastAPI backend foundation + WebSocket connection, without changing Ben/Julie behavior yet.
